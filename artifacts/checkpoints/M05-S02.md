# M05-S02 Checkpoint — Delivery Rules and Server-side Quote Engine

**Status:** `REVIEW`

## Result

M05-S02 replaces the delivery readiness placeholder with seller-managed Nepal delivery rules and introduces a server-owned, opaque, time-limited quote boundary. Delivery rules are authenticated seller administration only. Quotes are internal application-service behavior: no public quote endpoint, checkout session, reservation, payment configuration, customer data, or order aggregate was introduced.

## Scope completed

- Tenant-owned `DeliveryRule` and `DeliveryRuleZone` aggregates with bounded normalized Nepal district, municipality, and locality coverage.
- Flat and threshold NPR fees, optional ETA, COD delivery eligibility, active/inactive state, deterministic locality → municipality → district matching, priority and stable tie-breaking.
- Authenticated seller `GET`, `POST`, and `PUT` delivery-rule endpoints under `/v1/store/delivery-rules`, with verified tenant context, Storefront permissions, optimistic concurrency, creation idempotency, and privacy-safe audit events.
- Real delivery readiness: an active rule clears `delivery_not_configured`; payment remains an activation blocker.
- Internal `IStorefrontQuoteService` using canonical published Catalog pricing, Store publication visibility, current Inventory availability, matching delivery rules, all-server-owned NPR totals, and an opaque ten-minute Data Protection token.
- Forward-only EF migration `20260904180120_AddDeliveryRulesAndQuoteEngine`, OpenAPI snapshot, generated TypeScript contract, domain/integration/authorization/OpenAPI coverage.

Deferred exactly as planned: public host/store resolution and public quote route (M05-S05), checkout/reservation (M05-S03), orders (M05-S04), payment configuration, and frontend mutation wiring (M05-S06).

## Security and scope review

- Delivery-rule endpoints require authenticated identity, verified tenant context, and `storefront.read`/`storefront.write`; foreign rules resolve as not found.
- Rule-creation audit metadata contains only the active/COD state; it excludes zone details, ETA, idempotency keys, and customer data.
- Quote tokens are purpose-scoped, opaque, time-limited, and reject tampering. They do not reserve stock or authorise an order.
- No public endpoint exposes tenant IDs, quotes, delivery rules, customer contact details, or payment facts.
