## Scope completed

1. **ADR-022 `Proposed`**: ownership semantics end to end. It amends ADR-017: an Instagram-app reply takes the conversation over. Your approval of this ADR is the milestone's review requirement ("no bot message after takeover").
2. **Send guard** (`AssistantSendGuard`). It runs **before the model is called and again right before enqueue**, and checks:
   - the connection is active and can send text;
   - customer safety (not spam, identity not erased);
   - entitlement (operator allowlist);
   - ownership: automated, the trigger arrived after the last release, and no newer customer message;
   - readiness.

   Readiness gained the required check `ai_entitled`.
3. **Inbound trigger.**
   - Customer messages schedule a turn **after the webhook transaction commits**, with a 4 s debounce. Only shops the assistant may serve get jobs.
   - `AssistantTurnJob` runs in the trigger's tenant as the system. Busy results are rescheduled with backoff (5·2ⁿ s, up to 6 times); a crashed turn restarts under its turn key.
   - The per-minute **sweeper** reschedules unanswered messages that lost their trigger (30-minute window). It reads only entitled shops with an enabled policy, filtered in SQL.
4. **Native-app reply takeover.** An echo of a reply typed in the Instagram app is checked 30 s later. If no Kreyora send matches it, the conversation is taken over: audited `native_app_reply`, queued assistant replies cancelled.
5. **Release** records `AutomationResumedAt`. Messages from before the release stay with the person.
6. **Staff queue.** `GET /v1/conversations?needsPerson=true`: owned by a person, open, customer waiting; oldest wait first. List items gain `escalationCategory` and `waitingSince`.

## Ownership semantics for your approval (ADR-022)

- **Who owns a chat:** the assistant (`Automated`) or a person (`HumanTakeover`).
  - **A person takes it by:** a staff takeover; a staff reply; the assistant's escalation; a reply typed in the Instagram app.
  - **It goes back only by** an explicit, audited release.
- **The guarantee: no assistant message *starts* sending after a takeover commits.**
  - The claim (`Queued → Sending`) is the linearization point.
  - The takeover cancels queued automation messages in its own transaction.
  - The delivery job re-reads ownership right before the claim, and a takeover committing in between wins on the row version.
- **The only exceptions:**
  - a message already claimed (in flight); it is reported in the takeover audit as `automationMessagesInFlight`;
  - the assistant's own hand-off notice.
- **Proof (tests):**
  - a takeover before the model call, during it, after enqueue, and **between the delivery gate read and the claim**: no send, no delivery attempt;
  - after the claim: delivered and reported as in flight.

## Security and isolation review

- **Authentication/authorization:**
  - `needsPerson` uses the existing `conversations.read` policy;
  - release uses `conversations.write` (Viewer 403 tested).
- **Background jobs:**
  - they run as the system inside the tenant from the job envelope, re-resolved by the job runner;
  - suspended or archived tenants do not run;
  - conversations and messages are re-checked inside the tenant.
- **Tenant isolation:**
  - the sweeper is the only cross-tenant read: IDs only, and every scheduled job re-checks in its own tenant;
  - tested: a foreign conversation is not found, a foreign echo is invisible, a suspended tenant gets no turn.
- **Secrets and PII:**
  - logs carry IDs, outcomes and counts only;
  - live-session evidence has no message text, account IDs, tunnel addresses or secrets;
  - secret values were read in-process only, never printed;
  - live AI received made-up content only (ADR-018 synthetic allowlist).
- **Abuse, idempotency, concurrency:**
  - duplicate deliveries and re-sent mids give one message and one turn;
  - the turn key replays finished turns; busy turns back off;
  - the takeover-versus-claim race is decided by the row version.
- **Kill switches:** `Ai:Enabled`; remove a shop from `Ai:Entitlements`; the shop's assistant toggle; the conversation takeover.
