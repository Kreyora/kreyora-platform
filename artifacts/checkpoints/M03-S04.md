## Implementation design

- **Permission authority:** `TenantPermissions` is the single matrix used by the ASP.NET Core authorization handler and Infrastructure services. Roles are read from the verified request context, never cookie role claims.
- **Support access:** a support user requires a current global `PlatformSupport` role plus an unrevoked, unexpired Owner grant with a reason and maximum eight-hour expiry. A support context is not a membership and cannot modify tenant-owned data.
- **Audit:** immutable `AuditEvent` rows hold tenant, actor, optional support actor/grant, action, target, UTC time, reason, correlation ID, and redacted JSON metadata. EF rejects modifications and deletions. Membership mutations audit when they execute in an authenticated tenant context; support grant mutations and support history access are audited.

## Contracts and persistence

- **API:** authenticated tenant-scoped `GET /v1/permissions`, `GET /v1/audit-events?cursor=&pageSize=`, Owner-only `POST /v1/support-access-grants`, and `DELETE /v1/support-access-grants/{id}`.
- **Migration:** `20260801220741_AddPolicyRbacAndAuditEvents` creates `audit_events` and `support_access_grants` plus tenant-aligned indexes. It applied successfully to the local development PostgreSQL database.
- **ADR:** [ADR-005](../../docs/decisions/ADR-005-platform-support-audit-access.md) records the no-membership, Owner-issued, read-only, eight-hour model.
